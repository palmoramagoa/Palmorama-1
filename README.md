# Palmorama Retreat Home

**Mandrem · Goa**

A tranquil farmhouse woven into the wild — suites, a dome stay, and a natural pool, held quietly by the palms.

---

## About

Palmorama is a serene farmhouse retreat in Mandrem, North Goa, where nature and comfort meet. Set among lush greenery, the property offers thoughtful stays for couples, families, and anyone seeking a quiet escape — from elegant suites to a one-of-a-kind geodesic dome.

Unwind by a chemical-free natural pool, share slow mornings at the garden café, and begin the day with yoga in the Sacred Shala. For deeper rest and renewal, **Srot** — the wellness wing at Palmorama — holds space for movement, sound healing, and holistic living.
